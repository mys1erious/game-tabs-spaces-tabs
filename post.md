# First reply

Left half or Tab for a tab, right half or Space for a space. Wait too long and the editor auto-indents for you by copying the line above, which also counts. A self-playing run gets to 3,534 lines: https://mys1erious.github.io/game-tabs-spaces-tabs/?play

# Why this shape

The reference works because a two-beat rhythm you can't stop meets one slapstick punishment; tabs vs spaces is the coder's own two beats, and the road of thousands of lines gives the same "how far does this go" pull before the slap.
